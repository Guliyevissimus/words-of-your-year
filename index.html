Source: Google Books Ngram data (2019 version). Each line shows how often a word appeared per million words in books of that language, smoothed over five years, 1800 to 2019. A word is listed under the year its use peaked, if it rose clearly above earlier levels and has since fallen. Names and places are left out where the language capitalises them.

English lists were reviewed by hand. The notes on why a word peaked were written with Claude, an AI model, and can contain mistakes. Lists for the other languages are picked automatically and have no notes.
