# Words of Your Year

Type a year and see the words whose use in books peaked that year. English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Russian, Hebrew and Chinese.

Data: Google Books Ngram (2019 version), 1800 to 2019. English lists reviewed by hand, with notes written with Claude. Other languages are picked automatically.

Live site: https://guliyevissimus.github.io/words-of-your-year/
